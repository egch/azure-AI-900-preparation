## Microsoft Guiding principles for responsive AI
### Accountability
>AI accountability refers to the idea that artificial intelligence should be developed, deployed, and utilized such that responsibility for bad outcomes can be assigned to liable parties
### Reliability and Safety
>Reliability and safety in a Responsible AI framework refer to the assurance that AI systems will perform consistently, accurately, and safely across a variety of conditions and in the face of unexpected situations. 
### Privacy and Security
>Protecting user privacy and ensuring the security of AI systems are key principles for Microsoft. This involves implementing robust measures to safeguard user data and prevent unauthorized access.
### Transparency
>Transparency is crucial in responsible AI. Microsoft aims to provide transparency in how its AI systems operate, allowing users and stakeholders to understand the decision-making processes and outcomes.
### Inclusiveness
>Inclusiveness is about ensuring that AI technologies are designed to benefit all users, irrespective of their backgrounds or abilities. Microsoft seeks to avoid creating technologies that unintentionally exclude or disadvantage certain groups.
### Fairness
>Microsoft emphasizes the importance of ensuring that AI systems are designed and implemented in a way that is fair and unbiased. This involves addressing and mitigating biases in data and algorithms to avoid discriminatory outcomes.
